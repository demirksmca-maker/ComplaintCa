Can a US Business Use Small Claims Court in Canada?

Published November 6, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: yes, if the amount fits the province's limit and the case belongs there. The limit is $50,000 in Ontario, $100,000 in Alberta, $35,000 in British Columbia (claims up to $5,000 go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal) and $15,000 in Québec, where only companies with 10 or fewer employees can file.

Small claims courts are built for people and businesses without lawyers, and they are much cheaper than a full lawsuit. A US business can use them too, as long as the amount fits and the case belongs in that province.

Which province?

Usually the province where the Canadian company is based, or where the contract was made or carried out. If your contract names a court or requires arbitration, that clause normally decides.

Suing from outside Canada

1You can file from abroad. You do not need to live in Canada to file, but you may need to attend hearings. Ask the court whether you can join by video.

2Security for costs. In Ontario's Superior Court, a plaintiff who lives outside Ontario can be ordered to post money as security for costs. Check the court's own rules before you file.

3Getting advice. The Law Society of Ontario's free referral service is only for Ontario residents; non-residents can use its lawyer and paralegal directory.

If you are owed more than the limit

You can claim more and give up the extra, or file in the province's superior court. In British Columbia, the amount you give up cannot be claimed anywhere else later, so decide before you file.

Frequently asked

What is the small claims limit in Ontario?

$50,000 for claims filed on or after October 1, 2025, not counting interest and costs.

Can a company use Québec small claims court?

Only if it had 10 or fewer employees in the 12 months before filing. The limit there is $15,000.

What is the limit in British Columbia?

Claims up to $5,000 go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal, and Provincial Court small claims handles $5,001 to $35,000.

What is the limit in Alberta?

The Alberta Court of Justice hears civil claims up to $100,000. Larger claims go to the Court of King's Bench.
