How to Write a Demand Letter to a Canadian Company (Free Template)

Published November 6, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026 · 3 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: state who owes what, list the invoices, give a clear payment deadline (often 10 to 15 days), say what you will do next, and send it in a way you can prove, such as courier or registered mail plus email.

A demand letter is the cheapest tool you have. It shows you are serious, it creates a paper trail for court, and it often ends the dispute on its own.

What to include

1The exact legal name. Use the name from the corporate registry, not only the trade name.

2What you supplied. Goods or services, and the contract or purchase order they came under.

3The amount. Invoice numbers, dates, the total and the currency.

4A deadline. A specific date, often 10 to 15 days away.

5The next step. Court, arbitration or a collection agency. Only say what you will really do.

Free template

[Your company name and address] [Date] [Debtor's exact legal name] [Debtor's address] Re: Unpaid invoices [numbers], total [amount and currency] We supplied [goods or services] under [contract or purchase order, date]. Invoices [numbers] for a total of [amount and currency] were due on [date] and remain unpaid. Please pay the full amount by [date, usually 10 to 15 days from today] to [payment details]. If we do not receive payment by that date, we intend to [file a claim in court / refer the debt to collection] without further notice, and to claim interest and costs where allowed. [Name, title, signature]

How to send it

Send it by courier or registered mail so you have proof of delivery, and also by email to the person you deal with. Keep a copy of everything. If they reply admitting the debt, save that reply: it can matter for the deadline.

If the company is in Québec

In Québec this letter is called a mise en demeure, and it is a normal step before a claim. The general deadline to sue is 3 years, and a company can use small claims (up to $15,000) only if it had 10 or fewer employees in the past 12 months.

Frequently asked

Is a demand letter required before suing in Canada?

It is not always required, but it is the normal first step, and it gives you written proof that you asked for payment before filing.

How long should the payment deadline be?

A specific date 10 to 15 days away is common. Shorter can look unreasonable; longer slows you down.

Does a lawyer need to send it?

No. A letter from your business works. A lawyer's letter can add pressure on larger debts.

What is a mise en demeure?

It is the Québec name for a formal demand letter. It is a normal step before filing a claim in Québec.
