Canadian Passport: What Documents Do You Need to Apply?

Published November 24, 2026 · Updated November 24, 2026 · 4 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: For a new adult passport in Canada you need form PPTC 153, at least 1 valid ID, proof of Canadian citizenship, 2 identical passport photos, a guarantor and 2 references. A 10-year passport costs $163.50. It takes 10 business days in person at a passport office, or 20 by mail. If a complete application takes over 30 business days, your fee is refunded automatically.

Quick steps

  1. Download the latest form from Canada.ca: PPTC 153 for adults (16+), PPTC 155 for children. Old forms can be rejected.
  2. Gather your ID, proof of citizenship and 2 identical photos.
  3. Ask a guarantor to sign, and list 2 references.
  4. Apply in person at a passport office (10 business days) or by mail (20 business days). Pay the fee.
  5. Track it online. Past 30 business days? The fee is refunded automatically. Bad service? Send a complaint to IRCC.

The document checklist (new adult passport)

  • Form: PPTC 153, Adult General Passport Application (in Canada or the USA).
  • ID: at least 1 valid identity document. The original or a photocopy of both sides.
  • Proof of citizenship: a birth certificate if born in Canada, or a citizenship certificate if born abroad.
  • Photos: 2 identical photos that meet the Canada.ca passport photo rules.
  • Guarantor + 2 references.
  • Old passport: if it is still valid, send it with the application.

Child passport: since November 5, 2024, proof of parentage is needed every time. Separated or divorced parents must also send all original custody documents, every time.

Who can be your guarantor?

  • A Canadian citizen, 18 or older.
  • Has known you personally for at least 2 years.
  • Holds a 5- or 10-year Canadian passport that is valid, or expired less than 1 year ago.
  • A family member or someone you live with is fine.

No guarantor who has known you 2 years? Fill in form PPTC 132 (statutory declaration) and swear it before an authorized person. It can slow things down. Renewing? You don't need a guarantor.

Fees and processing time

Fees in Canada (since March 31, 2026):

  • Adult, 10 years: $163.50
  • Adult, 5 years: $122.50
  • Child (5 years): $58.50

Processing time:

  • In person at a passport office: 10 business days.
  • By mail or at a regular Service Canada Centre: 20 business days (mailing time not included).
  • Need it sooner? Urgent pick-up (end of next business day) or express (2 to 9 business days) at offices that offer it. Extra fees apply.

Passport late? Refund and complaint

30 days or free: if a complete application takes more than 30 business days to process, the passport fee is refunded. You don't have to ask: it is automatic. The clock starts when a complete application arrives and stops when the passport is printed. Mailing time does not count.

Complete means: the filled-in form, every required document, photos that meet the rules and the full fee.

Bad service? Send a complaint through the IRCC feedback form. No refund by July 1 of the next fiscal year? Contact IRCC.

Frequently Asked

What documents do I need for a new Canadian passport?

Form PPTC 153, at least 1 valid ID, proof of Canadian citizenship (birth or citizenship certificate), 2 identical passport photos, a guarantor and 2 references.

How much is a Canadian passport in 2026?

Since March 31, 2026, applying in Canada: $163.50 for a 10-year adult passport, $122.50 for 5 years and $58.50 for a child passport.

How long does a Canadian passport take?

10 business days in person at a passport office, 20 business days by mail or at a regular Service Canada Centre. Mailing time is not included.

Can a family member be my passport guarantor?

Yes, if they are a Canadian citizen, 18 or older, have known you for at least 2 years and hold an eligible Canadian passport.

What if my passport takes longer than 30 business days?

Under the 30 days or free guarantee, the fee is refunded automatically if a complete application takes more than 30 business days to process.
