Canadian client won't pay your invoice? Here's the path.

A US business can recover money from a Canadian company in 5 steps. Start cheap, escalate only if needed.

1

Tell us your case

Who owes you, how much, which province they are in, and do you have a contract or invoice?

2

Gather proof

Contract, invoices, emails, delivery records. Check the contract for an arbitration or jurisdiction clause.

3

Send a demand letter

A formal letter with the amount, a deadline, and what happens next. Many disputes end here.

4

Choose your route

Based on the amount and your contract:

Small claims courtSmaller amounts, no lawyer needed
Arbitration / mediationIf the contract says so
Collection agencyThey chase, take a cut
Superior courtLarge amounts, use a lawyer
5

Get paid

Win or settle, then enforce: garnishment, seizure, or registering a US judgment in Canada.
