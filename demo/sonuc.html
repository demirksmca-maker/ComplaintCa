Canadian Client Won't Pay Your Invoice? Here's the Path

Test page · Roadmap draft · 2 min read

Short answer: a US business can recover money from a Canadian company in 5 steps: tell us the case, gather proof, send a demand letter, choose a route, then enforce. Start cheap and escalate only if needed.

The Roadmap
→Your Case →Proof →Demand Letter →Your Route →Get Paid →Start Now

1. Tell us your case

1Who owes you? Company name and the province it is in.

2How much? The unpaid amount and the invoice dates.

3What did you agree? A signed contract, a purchase order, or just emails.

↑ Back to sections

2. Gather proof

Contract, invoices, emails, delivery records and any payment promises. Check the contract for an arbitration or jurisdiction clause: it can decide where the dispute must be heard.

↑ Back to sections

3. Send a demand letter

A formal letter with the amount owed, a clear deadline, and what you will do next. Many disputes end here, before any fees.

↑ Back to sections

4. Choose your route

RouteBest for
Small claims courtSmaller amounts, no lawyer needed
Arbitration / mediationWhen the contract requires it
Collection agencyThey chase the debt and take a cut
Superior courtLarge amounts, with a lawyer
↑ Back to sections

5. Get paid

Win or settle, then enforce: garnishment, seizure, or registering a US judgment in a Canadian court.
