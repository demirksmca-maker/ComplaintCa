Canada Sheds 4,700 Jobs; Unemployment Hits Six-Month High of 6.7%

Economists expected a gain of about 13,600. Private employers cut 52,500 jobs, and youth unemployment rose to 13.7%.

Economy Desk · November 6, 2026 · 4 min read

Unemployment rate: 6.9% in April, 6.5% in September, 6.7% in October. Source: Statistics Canada.

Canada's labour market weakened again in October. Statistics Canada says employment fell by about 4,700, and the unemployment rate rose to 6.7% from 6.5% in September. That's the highest since April.

The private sector did the damage: it lost 52,500 jobs, partly offset by 49,600 new public-sector jobs. The employment rate slipped to 60.5%.

Young people felt it most. Unemployment for those aged 15 to 24 climbed to 13.7%, while the rate for workers aged 25 to 54 held steady. Job losses for 2026 so far add up to about 46,000.

The weak report lands a week after the Bank of Canada raised rates. Its next decision is December 9.
