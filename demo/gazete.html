Budget 2026 · Ottawa

A $62.3-billion deficit. Here is what reaches your wallet.

Ottawa tabled its budget on Nov 5. Cheaper gas until January, a bigger grocery cheque in early 2027, and a sovereign wealth fund. Then the loonie had its worst week since September.

The budget adds $142.1 billion in new measures over five years. The government says about two-thirds aim at growing the economy and the rest at the cost of living. The parliamentary budget officer expects a bigger deficit than Ottawa does: $63.3 billion this year.

For households, the big items are simple. The federal fuel excise tax stays off until January 31, 2027, worth up to 10¢ a litre on gasoline and 4¢ on diesel. And a proposed one-time top-up to the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit would pay up to $679 more to a couple with two children in early 2027. It is a proposal, so it still has to pass.

Sovereign fund

$25B “Canada Strong Fund” takes shape

A Crown corporation investing only in Canada. Retail “Canada Strong Notes” are planned for early 2027.

SCAM WATCH

“Just install this app.” Don’t.

In Toronto, a caller posing as a phone company got a resident to install UltraViewer, a remote-control app, then guided them through online banking on a video call. Edmonton police spotted it and Toronto officers stopped a $2.9-million loss at the door.

STEP 1Hang up.

Your phone or internet company will not cold-call you to fix your computer.

STEP 2Install nothing.

No remote-access app, ever, for a caller you did not contact first.

STEP 3Call back.

Use the number on your bill or card, not one the caller gives you.

How to report a scam in Canada, step by step →
How the call works

Five moves, every time

  1. A friendly call from “your phone company”, a better plan or a billing problem.
  2. A reason to get into your device: a refund, a fix, a security check.
  3. A request to install a remote-access app. Now they see your screen.
  4. A video call while you “just log in to your bank”.
  5. Pressure to keep going, fast, and to tell no one.
Real companies never

Any one of these = scam

  • Ask you to install remote-access software
  • Ask you to open online banking while they watch
  • Send a courier to pick up a phone, card or cash
  • Ask for gift cards, crypto or a Bitcoin machine
  • Tell you to keep it from your bank or family
Say this: “I’ll call you back on the number on my bill.” Then hang up.
Already let them in?

Disconnect. Call your bank. Then report it.

Turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the computer, and uninstall the app. Call your bank on the number on your card to freeze accounts. Then report to local police and the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at 1-888-495-8501.
