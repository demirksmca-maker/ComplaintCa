Ontario — Gyms & Memberships

Gym and fitness memberships count as personal development services under Ontario's Consumer Protection Act. You can cancel within 10 days of getting your written contract, with no reason needed.

After that, cancel in writing and keep proof. If the business keeps charging you or ignores you, file a complaint.

Consumer Protection Ontario — 1-800-889-9768 →

Quebec — Office de la protection du consommateur

Quebec law lets you cancel a fitness or gym contract at any time. If services have started, the gym can only keep a small, capped penalty plus what you already used.

Send a written notice of cancellation. If the gym refuses, contact the OPC.

Office de la protection du consommateur (OPC) →

Phone, Internet & TV Contracts

Under the CRTC's codes you can cancel phone, internet and TV service at any time. You may still owe what is left on a device you financed.

If your provider won't cancel or keeps billing you, complain to them first, then to the CCTS for free.

CCTS Online Complaint Form — 1-888-221-1687 → Phone, Internet & TV Complaint Guide →

Still Being Charged After You Cancelled

Call your credit card company or bank. Ask them to stop the recurring payment and to dispute charges made after you cancelled. Send them your cancellation proof.

Free trials that turned into paid plans without clear notice can also be reported to the Competition Bureau.

Quick Answer

Cancel in writing (email or registered mail) and keep proof. If you're still charged, ask your bank or card company to stop the payment and dispute the charges. Then complain to your provincial consumer office: Consumer Protection Ontario (1-800-889-9768), the OPC in Quebec, Consumer Protection BC (1-888-564-9963) or Service Alberta (1-877-427-4088). Phone, internet and TV disputes go to the CCTS.

How to Cancel the Right Way

  • Read your contract for the cancellation steps and notice period.
  • Cancel in writing: email, the company's online form, or registered mail. Screenshots count.
  • Write down the date, the name of anyone you spoke to, and any confirmation number.
  • Watch your next statement. If a charge shows up, call your card company right away.
  • For app store subscriptions (Apple, Google), cancel in the app store settings, not just in the app.

Frequently Asked

Can I cancel a gym membership in Ontario?

Yes. Gym memberships are personal development services under Ontario's Consumer Protection Act, so you can cancel within 10 days of getting the written contract. After that, follow the contract's cancellation terms and complain to Consumer Protection Ontario (1-800-889-9768) if the gym won't cooperate.

Can I cancel a gym contract in Quebec?

Yes, at any time. Quebec's Consumer Protection Act limits what the gym can keep to a small penalty plus the services you already used. Contact the Office de la protection du consommateur if the gym refuses.

My subscription keeps charging me after I cancelled. What do I do?

Ask your bank or credit card company to stop the recurring payment and dispute the charges made after you cancelled. Send them proof that you cancelled.

Where do I complain if my phone or internet provider won't cancel?

Complain to the provider first. If it isn't fixed, file with the Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS) for free, at 1-888-221-1687.

Who do I report a free-trial subscription trap to?

Misleading free trials and hidden fees can be reported to the Competition Bureau. Also dispute the charges with your card company.

Do I have to complain to the business first?

Yes. Consumer offices and the CCTS expect you to give the business a chance to fix it first. Put it in writing and keep a copy.
