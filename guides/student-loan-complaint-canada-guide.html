1. NSLSC — National Student Loans Service Centre

The NSLSC doesn't publish a public email — contact it through the secure online messaging system in your account, or by phone. Keep a copy of every message you send.

NSLSC Contact — 1-888-815-4514 →

2. Service Canada / ESDC (If Unresolved)

Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) oversees the NSLSC. Escalate here if the NSLSC's own messaging system isn't resolving your issue.

Service Canada — Office for Client Satisfaction — 1-866-506-6806 →

Quebec: a Separate System

Quebec doesn't use the NSLSC at all — it runs its own program, Aide financière aux études (AFE). To challenge a decision, ask AFE's Bureau de la révision for a review (form 1073) within 3 years of the decision — not the NSLSC.

AFE — Complaints & review requests (client services 1-877-643-3750) →

Frequently Asked

How do I file a complaint against the NSLSC?

Contact the NSLSC through its secure online messaging system or by phone. If unresolved, escalate to Service Canada / ESDC, which oversees the NSLSC.

Is there an ombudsman for Canada Student Loans?

No dedicated ombudsman exists. Unresolved disputes escalate to Service Canada / ESDC, or to your MP's office, which can request a status update on a stuck file.

How is Quebec's student loan system different?

Quebec runs its own program, Aide financière aux études (AFE), instead of the NSLSC. Complaints and requests for review go to AFE, not the NSLSC.

What if the NSLSC made a billing or repayment error?

Document it with your account statements and contact the NSLSC's secure messaging system first. Keep every message — you'll need the record if you escalate.

How do I contact the NSLSC by phone or email?

Call the National Student Loans Service Centre at 1-888-815-4514, or use the secure messaging system in your NSLSC online account. The NSLSC doesn't publish a public email address, so keep a copy of every message you send.
