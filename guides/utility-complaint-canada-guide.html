Ontario

The Ontario Energy Board (OEB) covers electricity and natural gas utilities, sub-meter providers, and energy retailers — after you've contacted your utility directly first.

oeb.ca/consumer-protection →

British Columbia

The BC Utilities Commission (BCUC) — you must first try to resolve the issue with the utility directly, and BCUC can escalate the file for review by a commissioner if it remains unresolved.

bcuc.com/WhatWeDo/ComplaintProcess →

Alberta

The Alberta Utilities Commission (AUC) handles compliance and enforcement issues, including unfair or incorrect utility rate charges.

auc.ab.ca/enforcement-make-complaint →

Quebec

File with Hydro-Québec's complaints office first. An appeal to the Régie de l'énergie carries a review fee, and its decision is final.

regie-energie.qc.ca →

Manitoba

The Manitoba Public Utilities Board (PUB) handles complaints about electricity and gas utilities in the province.

pubmanitoba.ca →

Saskatchewan and Other Provinces

Saskatchewan's power and gas utilities (SaskPower, SaskEnergy) are Crown corporations without a separate independent complaints commission in the same structure as OEB or BCUC — if their own complaints process doesn't resolve things, Ombudsman Saskatchewan can review how the Crown corporation handled your complaint. Ask Asya for the current contact in your specific province.

Always contact your utility first

Every regulator above expects you to have raised it with your utility first. Nunavut's Utility Rates Review Council advises on rates and doesn't publish a complaint process — start with Qulliq Energy.

Required First Step: Your Utility's Own Process

Every regulator above expects you to have already filed a complaint directly with your electricity or gas provider and given them a chance to respond. Keep your account number, the dates you contacted them, and their response — you'll need it to escalate.

Frequently Asked

Who do I complain to about my electricity bill in Ontario?

Your utility first, then the Ontario Energy Board if unresolved.

Who regulates utility complaints in BC?

The BC Utilities Commission, after you've tried resolving it with the utility directly.

Who regulates utility complaints in Alberta?

The Alberta Utilities Commission, including unfair or incorrect rate charges.

How do I complain about Hydro-Québec?

File with Hydro-Québec's complaints office first, then appeal to the Régie de l'énergie, whose decision is final.

Do I have to contact my utility before the regulator?

Yes, in every province — regulators expect proof you tried resolving it directly first.

What is ComplaintCA?

A Canadian civic platform that identifies the correct authority for a complaint, drafts it with AI in 7 languages, and helps track it through to resolution.

I'm Deaf or hard of hearing — how do I call a phone-only authority?

Use Canada VRS (free sign-language video relay, available 24/7) or dial 711 for a text-based relay operator — both let you place a real phone call to any number listed on this page.
