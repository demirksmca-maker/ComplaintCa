Your MPP / MLA / MNA (Provincial)

Each province runs its own legislature website with a "find your member" search — for example Ontario's Legislative Assembly (ola.org) or BC's Legislative Assembly (leg.bc.ca). Search "[your province] legislative assembly find my MLA" if you're unsure of the exact site.

Your City Councillor (Municipal)

Search your city or town's official website for its council or ward directory — most let you search by address. ComplaintCA's tool pulls this automatically using the Represent API, a public, nonpartisan database of Canadian elected officials at all three levels.

Frequently Asked

How do I find out who my MP is?

Use ourcommons.ca/members/en, or ComplaintCA's Find My Representative tool, and enter your address or postal code.

How do I find my MPP?

If you're in Ontario, search the Legislative Assembly of Ontario's member directory at ola.org, or use ComplaintCA's Find My Representative tool.

How do I find my MLA by postal code?

Enter your postal code into ComplaintCA's Find My Representative tool, or your province's legislative assembly website.

What's the difference between an MP, MPP, MLA, and MNA?

MP is federal. MPP (Ontario), MLA (most provinces), and MNA (Quebec) are all the same thing — your provincial representative.

Should I contact my MP or my city councillor?

Depends on the issue: MPs handle federal programs like immigration and EI; councillors handle property tax, zoning, and local bylaws.

Can my MP or MPP actually solve my problem?

They can't override a department's decision, but their office can escalate a stuck file or request a status update — often faster than a general call centre.

How do I find my city councillor?

Search your municipality's website for its council/ward directory, or use ComplaintCA's Find My Representative tool.

Who is my provincial representative?

Your provincial representative is your MPP in Ontario, your MNA in Quebec, or your MLA in most other provinces. Enter your postal code in ComplaintCA's Find My Representative tool, or search your province's legislative assembly website, to see their name and contact details.

What is ComplaintCA?

A Canadian civic platform that routes you to the correct authority, drafts complaints with AI in 7 languages, and includes a Find My Representative tool for all three levels of government.
