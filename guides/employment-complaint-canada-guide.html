Frequently Asked

Where do I file a complaint against a federally regulated employer?

The Federal Labour Program, at 1-800-641-4049.

Where do I file an employment standards complaint in Ontario?

The Employment Standards Information Centre, at 1-800-531-5551.

Does this cover workplace discrimination?

No — discrimination and harassment go to a human rights body, not an employment standards office.

What is the workplace complaint procedure in Canada?

First check whether your employer is federally regulated (banks, airlines, telecoms, interprovincial transport) — those complaints go to the Federal Labour Program at 1-800-641-4049. Every other employer falls under your province's employment standards office (in Ontario, 1-800-531-5551). Discrimination or harassment goes to a human rights body instead.

What is ComplaintCA?

A Canadian civic complaint platform that routes you to the correct authority, with AI-assisted drafting in 7 languages, an MP finder, a lawyer panel, and cyber crime reporting support.

I'm Deaf or hard of hearing — how do I call a phone-only authority?

Use Canada VRS (free sign-language video relay, available 24/7) or dial 711 for a text-based relay operator — both let you place a real phone call to any number listed on this page.
