Federally Regulated Employers or Services

Banks, airlines, telecoms, and other federally regulated employers/services, or federal government programs.

Canadian Human Rights Commission — 1-888-214-1090 →

Ontario

Discrimination or harassment by an Ontario employer, landlord, or service provider — including in Toronto, Ottawa, or anywhere else in the province — applications go directly to the Tribunal.

Human Rights Tribunal of Ontario (HRTO) — 1-866-598-0322 →

The Ontario Human Rights Commission (1-800-387-9080) provides policy guidance but does not process individual complaints directly.

Filing Deadlines

The Canadian Human Rights Commission generally requires complaints within 12 months of the incident, and Ontario's HRTO requires applications within 1 year of the incident (or of the last incident, if there was a series of similar incidents). Deadlines vary by province — file as soon as possible.

⏱ 12 months (federal) · 1 year (Ontario)

Frequently Asked

Where do I file a human rights complaint against a federal employer or service?

The Canadian Human Rights Commission, at 1-888-214-1090.

Where do I file a human rights complaint in Ontario?

Directly with the Human Rights Tribunal of Ontario (HRTO), at 1-866-598-0322.

Where do I file a human rights complaint in Toronto?

Toronto doesn't have its own human rights body — like the rest of Ontario, complaints go directly to the Human Rights Tribunal of Ontario (HRTO) at 1-866-598-0322.

Is there a time limit to file?

Generally 12 months (federal) or 1 year (Ontario HRTO) from the incident — file as soon as possible, as deadlines vary by province.

What is ComplaintCA?

A Canadian civic complaint platform that routes you to the correct authority, with AI-assisted drafting in 7 languages, an MP finder, a lawyer panel, and cyber crime reporting support.

I'm Deaf or hard of hearing — how do I call a phone-only authority?

Use Canada VRS (free sign-language video relay, available 24/7) or dial 711 for a text-based relay operator — both let you place a real phone call to any number listed on this page.
