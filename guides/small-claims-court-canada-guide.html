Ontario

$50,000

Ontario Small Claims Court (part of the Superior Court of Justice) — limit raised from $35,000 effective October 1, 2025.

ontario.ca — Small Claims Court →

British Columbia

$5,000 / $35,000

Claims up to $5,000 must go through the online Civil Resolution Tribunal (CRT). Claims from $5,001–$35,000 go to the Provincial Court of BC, Small Claims.

civilresolutionbc.ca (under $5,000) →
gov.bc.ca — Small Claims Court →

Alberta

$100,000

Alberta Court of Justice, Civil Claims — the highest limit in Canada, raised from $50,000 in August 2023.

albertacourts.ca — Civil Claims →

Quebec

$15,000

Small Claims Division of the Court of Québec (principal amount only — interest can push the award higher). Lawyers cannot represent you at the hearing itself.

quebec.ca — Small Claims →

Manitoba

$20,000

Manitoba Small Claims Court — limit increased to $20,000 effective January 1, 2025.

gov.mb.ca — Small Claims Court →

Saskatchewan

$50,000

Provincial Court of Saskatchewan, Small Claims — limit increased to $50,000 effective April 1, 2024.

sasklawcourts.ca — Small Claims →

Before You File: Send a Demand Letter

Almost every small claims court expects you to have asked the other side to pay first, in writing, with a clear deadline (usually 10 days). Keep a copy — you'll attach it to your claim. ComplaintCA's AI drafting tool can help you write this letter in the right format for your province.

Atlantic Canada, the Territories & Higher Amounts

Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, PEI, Newfoundland & Labrador, Yukon, NWT, and Nunavut each run their own small claims process with their own limit. Ask Asya above with your province named for the current figure. If your claim is above your province's limit, you can still file for the maximum and abandon the excess, or proceed in a higher court.

Frequently Asked

What is the small claims limit in Ontario?

$50,000 as of October 1, 2025 (up from $35,000), not including interest and costs.

What is the small claims limit in BC?

Up to $5,000 via the online Civil Resolution Tribunal; $5,001–$35,000 via BC Provincial Court Small Claims.

What is the small claims limit in Alberta?

$100,000 — the highest in Canada — through the Alberta Court of Justice, Civil Claims.

What is the small claims limit in Quebec?

$15,000 (principal only) through the Court of Québec's Small Claims Division. Lawyers can't represent you at the hearing.

Do I need a lawyer for small claims court?

No — it's designed for self-represented people. In Quebec, lawyers aren't permitted to represent you at the hearing at all.

Should I go to small claims court or keep complaining to the ombudsman?

Try the free ombudsman/regulator route first. Small claims makes sense once that's exhausted, or for disputes with no dedicated ombudsman.
