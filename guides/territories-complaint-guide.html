Police (RCMP)

Yukon, the Northwest Territories, and Nunavut are policed by the RCMP. Complaints about an RCMP officer's conduct go to the federal Civilian Review and Complaints Commission, generally within one year.

Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for the RCMP (CRCC) — 1-800-665-6878 →

Frequently Asked

Where do I file a consumer complaint in Yukon?

Yukon Consumer Services, at 867-667-5111.

Where do I file a consumer complaint in the Northwest Territories?

Consumer Affairs at the Department of Municipal and Community Affairs, at 867-767-9161 ext 21022.

Where do I file a consumer complaint in Nunavut?

Nunavut Consumer Affairs, toll-free at 1-866-223-8139.

Does Nunavut have an ombudsman?

No. Nunavut has no Ombudsman. For a young person's problem with a government service, the Representative for Children and Youth can help (1-855-449-8118).

How do I complain about the RCMP in the territories?

All three territories are policed by the RCMP. Complaints go to the Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for the RCMP (CRCC), generally within one year.

What is ComplaintCA?

A Canadian civic complaint platform that routes you to the correct authority, with AI-assisted drafting in 7 languages, an MP finder, a lawyer panel, and cyber crime reporting support.

I'm Deaf or hard of hearing — how do I call a phone-only authority?

Use Canada VRS (free sign-language video relay, available 24/7) or dial 711 for a text-based relay operator — both let you place a real phone call to any number listed on this page.
