Ontario — Condominium Authority Tribunal

Raise it with your condo board or property manager first. Most disputes over records, governance, or nuisances (noise, smoke, pets) can go to the Condominium Authority Tribunal (CAT), filed online.

Condominium Authority of Ontario — 1-800-854-9014 →

British Columbia — Civil Resolution Tribunal

Most strata property disputes go through the Civil Resolution Tribunal (CRT) — apply online or by email.

CRT Strata Property — 1-844-322-2292 →

Alberta

No dedicated condo tribunal. Disputes under the Condominium Property Act typically go through mediation, arbitration, or the Provincial or Court of King's Bench, depending on the amount and issue.

Quebec

No dedicated tribunal for co-ownership (copropriété) disputes. Claims up to $15,000 go through Small Claims Court (Cour du Québec); larger or more complex disputes go through Quebec Superior Court.

Other Provinces

Manitoba and Saskatchewan have condominium legislation but no dedicated tribunal — disputes generally go through the small claims or provincial court.

Atlantic Canada

Nova Scotia has a condominium dispute officer for minor disputes (by-law breaches, records requests) and binding arbitration for bigger ones, through Service Nova Scotia.

Nova Scotia — Condominium Dispute Officer (Service Nova Scotia) →

New Brunswick has no tribunal, but either side can ask the Director of Condominiums to appoint an arbitrator.

New Brunswick — Director of Condominiums (appoints arbitrators) — 1-888-621-9789 →

PEI has no condo dispute body — disputes go to the Supreme Court of PEI.

Newfoundland & Labrador allows mediation or arbitration if both sides agree; otherwise, the Supreme Court.

Territories

Yukon and the NWT have no government condo dispute body: complain in writing to the board, then mediation or arbitration, then the courts.

Frequently Asked

How do I file a complaint against my condo corporation in Ontario?

Raise it with the board or property manager first. Most records, governance, or nuisance disputes can go to the Condominium Authority Tribunal (CAT), filed online.

What is the Condominium Authority Tribunal (CAT)?

Ontario's online condo dispute tribunal, run by the Condominium Authority of Ontario — currently handles records requests, some governance issues, and nuisance disputes.

How do I resolve a strata dispute in BC?

Through the Civil Resolution Tribunal (CRT) — apply online via the Solution Explorer or email a completed application to info@crtbc.ca.

Does Alberta or Quebec have a condo tribunal?

No. Alberta disputes go through mediation, arbitration, or the courts. Quebec co-ownership disputes go through Small Claims Court (up to $15,000) or Superior Court.
