How to File a Complaint About Police Conduct in Canada

This is different from reporting a crime — this is about an officer's own conduct. Every province has an independent civilian body for exactly this.

By Province

Ontario

General conduct complaints go to the Office of the Independent Police Review Director (OIPRD), which screens and can investigate directly or refer back to the service.

OIPRD — 1-877-411-4773 →

British Columbia

The Office of the Police Complaint Commissioner (OPCC) oversees municipal police conduct complaints in BC.

OPCC — 1-877-999-8707 →

Alberta

General conduct complaints start with the Professional Standards Branch of the relevant police service, with appeals to the Law Enforcement Review Board (LERB). Alberta's Serious Incident Response Team (ASIRT) is separate — it investigates only serious injury or death involving police, not general conduct complaints.

Quebec

General police ethics complaints go to the Commissaire à la déontologie policière. The Bureau des enquêtes indépendantes (BEI) is separate — it investigates only incidents involving serious injury or death.

RCMP (any province)

The Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for the RCMP (CRCC) is the federal independent body for RCMP conduct, regardless of which province the incident occurred in.

CRCC — 1-800-665-6878 →

Frequently Asked

How do I file a complaint against a police officer in Canada?

File with your province's civilian oversight body — Ontario's OIPRD, BC's OPCC, or the equivalent. For the RCMP, file with the federal CRCC.

What's the difference between complaining about police and reporting a crime?

Reporting a crime asks police to investigate someone else. A police complaint is about an officer's own conduct — excessive force, rudeness, or improper procedure.

How do I complain about the RCMP?

File with the Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for the RCMP (CRCC) at 1-800-665-6878.

Is there a time limit to file a police complaint?

Most provincial bodies require filing within 6 to 12 months of the incident, though some allow later filing with an explanation.

What happens after I file a complaint against police?

The oversight body screens it, then investigates directly, refers it back to the service with oversight, or dismisses it. You're notified and can usually appeal a dismissal.
