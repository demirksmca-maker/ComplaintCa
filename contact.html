Contact ComplaintCA

Last updated: October 27, 2026

ComplaintCA is run by a small independent team in Canada. The fastest way to reach us is by email at complaintcaca@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within 2 business days.

01General

Questions about the platform

Not sure which authority handles your problem, or how a feature works? Write to us with a short description and the province you are in. We can point you to the right guide or office, but we cannot give legal advice or file a complaint on your behalf.

02Corrections

Found an error in a guide?

Phone numbers, deadlines, and office names change. If something in one of our guides or articles is out of date, send us the page link and, if you have it, the official source showing the correct information. Verified corrections are usually published within a week, and the page's "last updated" date is changed.

03Privacy

Your data and privacy requests

To access, correct, or delete information you entered on ComplaintCA, email us from the address you used and put "Privacy request" in the subject line. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days, as described in our Privacy Policy.

04Lawyers

Partner lawyer enquiries

Licensed lawyers in good standing can ask about the Lawyer Panel or Partner Lawyer status. Please include your name, firm, province of call, and practice areas. Details of the partner programme are shared only with applicants.

Before you write

Emergencies. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. ComplaintCA is not an emergency service.

Fraud in progress. Report it to your bank first, then to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (1-888-495-8501).

Sensitive details. Please do not email passwords, full card numbers, or your SIN. We will never ask for them.
