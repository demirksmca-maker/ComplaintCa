RBC Complaint in Canada? Steps, the 56-Day Rule and OBSI

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026 · 3 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: use RBC's own complaint process first. After 56 days, or once you get its final answer, take it to OBSI, which is free: 1-888-451-4519. After a final response you have up to 180 days.

Quick steps

  1. Contact RBC (your branch, the team you dealt with or RBC customer care) and explain the problem in writing. Get a reference number.
  2. Not satisfied? Ask RBC to send your appeal to the RBC Client Complaints Appeal Office (CCAO).
  3. RBC has 56 calendar days to deal with your complaint.
  4. Still not satisfied, or 56 days passed? Go to the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments (OBSI), free: 1-888-451-4519, within 180 days of RBC's final response.
  5. OBSI must give its written conclusion within 120 days of having all the information it needs.

Step 1: RBC's complaint process

Start with RBC's own process, in writing, and keep copies. RBC has 56 calendar days to deal with the complaint.

Some RBC divisions also offer an internal escalation to the RBC Office of the Ombudsman.

What to write in your complaint:

  • Your name, account type and the last 4 digits of the account or card.
  • What happened, in order, with dates and amounts.
  • Who you spoke to and any reference numbers.
  • One clear request: a refund of a set amount, a fee reversed, a correction.
  • Your proof: statements, letters, emails, screenshots.

Keep a copy of everything you send.

Step 2: OBSI

If RBC does not resolve it, take it to the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments (OBSI). Phone: 1-888-451-4519. Email: ombudsman@obsi.ca.

What OBSI can and cannot do: OBSI is free and can recommend compensation up to $350,000, but it cannot force RBC to pay, because its recommendations are not binding on you or RBC.

The two numbers that matter

  • 56 days. Once RBC has used up the 56 calendar days, you can go to OBSI.
  • 180 days. You have up to 180 calendar days to go to OBSI after RBC's final response.

Pages differ slightly on details. Confirm on obsi.ca.

If you live in Quebec

Quebec residents may complete the Autorité des marchés financiers complaint form instead. Check RBC's complaint pages for the route that applies to you.

Frequently Asked

How do I complain about RBC?

Use RBC's complaint process first. If it is not resolved, take it to OBSI at 1-888-451-4519.

How long does RBC have to answer?

56 calendar days. After that you can go to OBSI.

How long do I have to go to OBSI?

Up to 180 calendar days after RBC's final response.

Is OBSI free and binding?

It is free for you. Its recommendations are not binding on you or RBC.

What is the RBC Client Complaints Appeal Office?

It is RBC's most senior internal complaint office. RBC forwards your appeal there if you are not satisfied with the first answer. It is part of RBC, so OBSI is the next, independent step.
