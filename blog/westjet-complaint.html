How to File a WestJet Complaint (and Claim Compensation)

Published October 25, 2026 · 4 min read
30 days
Time you give WestJet to reply before you can go to the CTA

WestJet takes questions, concerns and complaints in writing through the contact form on its Share your feedback page. Its phone lines are for bookings and changes. Booked a package? WestJet Vacations has its own form.

Step 2: Put It in Writing

WestJet Vacations warns replies can take more than 30 days from November to April. Reply to your confirmation email to follow up instead of filing again.

Say what happened, when, what you already tried and exactly what you want back. Our complaint letter template shows the six parts every complaint needs.

Step 3: If They Say No

  1. Send your claim to the airline in writing. For a delay or cancellation, you have 1 year to ask for compensation, and the airline has 30 days to pay or explain why not.
  2. Keep proof: booking reference, boarding pass, receipts and every email or chat.
  3. No answer or a "no" after 30 days? File a free complaint with the Canadian Transportation Agency (CTA). The CTA can order compensation, refunds or expenses, but not pain and suffering or lost income.

The CTA has a large backlog, so file early. Read CTA Backlog: 987 Days and the Airline Complaint Guide.

Frequently Asked

How do I file a complaint with WestJet?

Use the contact form on the WestJet Share your feedback page. WestJet asks for complaints in writing; its phone lines are for bookings.

How much compensation can I get for a delayed WestJet flight?

For a delay within WestJet's control, a large airline owes $400 for 3–6 hours, $700 for 6–9 hours and $1,000 for 9 hours or more. Check the CTA for the latest amounts.

WestJet did not answer my complaint.

After 30 days with no answer, or after a refusal, file a free complaint with the Canadian Transportation Agency with proof you wrote to WestJet.
