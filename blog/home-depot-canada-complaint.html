Home Depot Canada Complaint: Returns, Orders and Where to Escalate

Published November 2, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: Home Depot Canada says items bought on homedepot.ca can be returned to any Canadian store within 90 days, subject to a list of exceptions. For return instructions you can call 1-800-628-0525. If a manager refuses, document it and move to your provincial consumer office, your card issuer or small claims court. Details and wording below.

90 days
Home Depot Canada’s stated return window for homedepot.ca purchases, with exceptions. For return instructions call 1-800-628-0525.

What is the Home Depot Canada return rule?

The company’s returns page says items purchased on homedepot.ca can be returned to any of its Canadian stores within 90 days of purchase, subject to its “Return Policy Exceptions”. Some items have a shorter window or extra conditions. Read the exceptions list for your product before you return it, and screenshot the page on the day you buy so you can show what the policy said.

What should I do before I go to the store?

  • Find the item in the exceptions list and note the days that apply
  • Bring the receipt or order confirmation, plus the packaging and accessories
  • Photograph the item and any fault, with the date visible if you can
  • If the item is large, ask by phone how the return works before you load it

What do I say if a return or refund is refused?

Ask which exception is being applied and where it is written, then ask for a manager. If it is not solved on the spot, send this by email or the online form:

“I bought [item] on [date], order or receipt [number]. On [date] [store] declined my return because [reason given]. The policy on homedepot.ca says [quote]. Please review my case and confirm by [date] whether the return will be accepted.”

Keep the reference number and the name of the person who answered.

If the problem is with an installer or contractor

Ask for the contract or work order, the name of the company that did the work and who is responsible if it is faulty. Keep every message. Our renovation complaint guide explains deposits, deficiencies and the Ontario steps in more detail.

If Home Depot will not fix it

  • Provincial consumer office: in Ontario, Consumer Protection Ontario at 1-800-889-9768.
  • Paid by credit card: ask your issuer about a chargeback. Send the order, the refusal and the policy page (see our chargeback guide).
  • Larger amounts: small claims court, explained in our Small Claims guide.
