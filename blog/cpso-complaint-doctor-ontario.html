Complaint About a Doctor in Ontario? How to File With the CPSO

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: file with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO), online or by phone. It is free and you do not need a lawyer. The CPSO looks at the doctor's conduct. It cannot order the doctor to pay you.

Before you file

Where it makes sense, try to resolve the problem with the doctor first. Then gather the doctor's name, the dates, and what happened, in order.

If it is about the hospital rather than the doctor, start with the hospital instead: hospital complaint steps.

How to file

You can start a complaint online or by phone. On the CPSO site, the complaints page has the form. You can also speak with a College representative before you decide.

CPSO phone: 1-800-268-7096. For sexual abuse complaints there is a dedicated line: 1-800-268-7096, ext. 629.

What happens next

The CPSO tells the doctor about the complaint. An investigator gathers information, and the doctor responds.

Possible outcomes include ordering the doctor to take courses, a verbal caution, or sending the concerns to the Ontario Physicians and Surgeons Discipline Tribunal.

↑ Back to sections

What the CPSO cannot do

A CPSO complaint is about discipline, not money. It can not make the doctor pay for lost wages or extra medical bills. Talk to a lawyer if you want compensation.

The CPSO covers licensed doctors only. For a nurse, contact the College of Nurses of Ontario.

Frequently Asked

How do I file a complaint against a doctor in Ontario?

Start a complaint with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario (CPSO), online or by phone at 1-800-268-7096. Filing is free.

Do I need a lawyer to complain to the CPSO?

No. The complaints process is designed so the public can use it without a lawyer.

Can the CPSO get me compensation?

No. It deals with the doctor's conduct and can order courses, give a caution or refer the doctor to a discipline tribunal. It cannot make the doctor pay you.

Who handles a complaint about a nurse?

The College of Nurses of Ontario. The CPSO only covers licensed doctors.
