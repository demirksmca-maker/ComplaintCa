OBSI Is Now Canada's Only Banking Ombudsman — Investigations Are Up 90%

Published September 11, 2026 · 3 min read
+90%
increase in OBSI banking investigations in 2025, the year it became the single ombudsman for every bank in Canada.

If your bank complaint has stalled, the landscape you're dealing with actually changed underneath you this year — and mostly for the better.

What's Actually Happening

In 2025, the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments (OBSI) became the single external complaints body (ECB) for all of Canada's banks, ending nearly two decades where banks could choose between competing dispute-resolution services. Total consumer inquiries rose to more than 26,000 — up almost 60% from the record set the year before — and OBSI opened over 6,100 investigations, a 90% jump. Banking case volumes more than doubled year over year.

What It Means If You're Filing Now

One ombudsman means one process regardless of which bank you're dealing with — no more checking which dispute body your specific bank happened to use. In 2025, more than 1,300 complaints ended with monetary compensation totalling about $5.8 million, with resolution rates in the 40–55% range in favour of consumers and median compensation between $500 and $3,000.
