WSIB Claim Denied in Ontario? How to Object and Appeal

Published November 23, 2026 · Updated November 23, 2026 · 4 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: send WSIB's Intent to Object form by the deadline in your decision letter: 30 days for return-to-work and work transition decisions, 6 months for most others. If WSIB keeps its decision, an Appeals Resolution Officer reviews it. Still no? Appeal to the WSIAT within 6 months of WSIB's final decision.

Quick steps

  1. Read your WSIB decision letter. It states your deadline.
  2. Send the Intent to Object form before that deadline: 30 days for return-to-work or work transition, 6 months for most other decisions.
  3. The decision-maker looks at it again. If the decision stands, an Appeals Resolution Officer decides.
  4. Still disagree? File a Notice of Appeal with the Workplace Safety and Insurance Appeals Tribunal (WSIAT) within 6 months of WSIB's final decision.
  5. Not in a union? The Office of the Worker Adviser helps for free.

Your deadline: 30 days or 6 months

  • 30 days: decisions about return to work or work transition, including re-employment.
  • 6 months: most other WSIB decisions, such as entitlement or benefits.
  • Mixed letter: if one letter covers issues with both deadlines, WSIB uses 6 months.

Missed it? Write to the decision-maker, ask for an extension and explain why you were late.

How to object

Use WSIB's Intent to Object form. WSIB must receive it by your deadline.

What to include:

  • Your claim number and the date of the decision.
  • Which part of the decision you disagree with.
  • Why, and the new proof you have: medical notes, witness statements, job details.

If the decision-maker does not change it, your objection goes to an Appeals Resolution Officer, whose decision is WSIB's final one.

Appeal to the WSIAT

The Workplace Safety and Insurance Appeals Tribunal (WSIAT) is independent from WSIB.

  • Deadline: 6 months from WSIB's final decision.
  • Form: WSIAT Notice of Appeal.
  • Late? WSIAT can extend the time limit.

Free help

  • Not in a union: the Office of the Worker Adviser, an Ontario government agency, helps for free at WSIB and WSIAT.
  • Union member: ask your union representative.

Frequently Asked

How long do I have to object to a WSIB decision?

30 days for return-to-work and work transition decisions. 6 months for most other decisions. Your letter states the deadline.

What form do I use to object?

WSIB's Intent to Object form. WSIB must receive it by the deadline.

Who reviews my objection?

First the decision-maker. If the decision stands, an Appeals Resolution Officer makes WSIB's final decision.

How long do I have to appeal to WSIAT?

6 months from WSIB's final decision, using the WSIAT Notice of Appeal.

Can I get free help with a WSIB appeal?

Yes. If you are not in a union, the Office of the Worker Adviser helps for free. Union members can ask their union.
