Best Buy Canada Complaint: Returns, Refunds and Where to Escalate

Published November 2, 2026 · 5 min read
1-866-237-8289
Best Buy Canada customer service (1-866-BEST-BUY). The Contact Us form on bestbuy.ca is the other route.

Short answer: for an in-store purchase, go back to the store first; for everything else call 1-866-237-8289 or use the Contact Us form on bestbuy.ca. Return windows differ by product, so check the policy for your category before you go. If Best Buy still says no, the next stops are your provincial consumer office, your card issuer and small claims court.

Check the return window for your product first

Best Buy’s return policy is not the same for every item. Some categories, such as cellular devices, have their own rules. Before you drive to the store, open the return and exchange policy on bestbuy.ca, find your product type and note the number of days and any conditions. Bring the receipt, all accessories and the packaging, and take photos of the item before you hand it over.

What to bring and what to write down

  • Receipt or order number, and the date of purchase
  • Model and serial number
  • Photos or video of the problem (the fault, not just the box)
  • Name of every employee you speak to, the date and the answer given

What do I say if a refund is refused?

Stay calm and ask three things: which policy rule is being applied, where it is written, and who can review the decision. A message you can adapt:

“I bought [product] on [date], order [number]. It [describe the fault]. I was told [reason for refusal]. Please show me the policy this is based on, or escalate my case to a manager. I am asking for [refund / replacement / repair] by [date].”

Ask for the manager’s name and a reference number. If you are on the phone, ask them to confirm the outcome by email.

If Best Buy will not fix it

  • Provincial consumer office: in Ontario, Consumer Protection Ontario at 1-800-889-9768.
  • Misleading price or advertising: report it to the Competition Bureau.
  • Paid by credit card: ask your issuer about a chargeback and send them your evidence (see our chargeback guide).
  • Larger amounts: small claims court. Our Small Claims guide explains the steps.

When the manufacturer is responsible

For a fault that appears during the warranty period, the maker’s warranty may be the right route. Ask Best Buy to tell you, in writing, whether it will handle the repair or whether you must contact the manufacturer. That answer matters if you later need to prove what you were told.

Frequently Asked

What is the Best Buy Canada customer service number?

1-866-237-8289 (1-866-BEST-BUY). You can also use the Contact Us form on bestbuy.ca.

Where do I take a Best Buy return?

Best Buy’s help page says in-store returns go to a local store. If you do not have a store near you, call customer service.

How many days do I have to return an item to Best Buy?

It depends on the product category. Check the return and exchange policy on bestbuy.ca for your item before you go.

Best Buy refused my refund. What next?

Ask for a manager and for the policy in writing, then go to your provincial consumer office, ask your card issuer about a chargeback, or consider small claims court.
