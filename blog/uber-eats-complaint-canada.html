How to File an Uber Eats Complaint in Canada

Published October 25, 2026 · 4 min read
48 hours
Report missing or wrong items within this window for the best chance of a refund

Uber Eats handles complaints inside the app. Uber says to report missing, wrong or never-delivered orders within 48 hours, after which it may not be able to refund you.

Step 1: Where to Complain

ChannelContact
In the appOrders → the order → Help
Websitehelp.uber.com → Uber Eats
Delivery person issueApp → Help → "I had an issue with my delivery person"

Step 2: Put It in Writing

Take a photo of what you got and the receipt in the bag before you eat or throw anything away. Uber asks for photos for wrong orders.

Say what happened, when, what you already tried and exactly what you want back. Our complaint letter template shows the six parts every complaint needs.

Step 3: If They Say No

  • App refused a refund for something you did not get: if you paid by credit card, ask your card issuer about a chargeback. Send the app's chat history with it.
  • Food made you sick: report the restaurant to your local public health unit, not only the app.
  • Pattern of unfair charges or fees: file with your provincial consumer office (Ontario: Consumer Protection Ontario, 1-800-889-9768).

Frequently Asked

How do I contact Uber Eats in Canada?

Open the app, go to Orders, pick the order and tap Help. Uber Canada directs all customer support through the app.

How long do I have to report an Uber Eats problem?

Report it within 48 hours. After that Uber says it may not be able to give a refund.

Uber Eats refused my refund.

Reply in the same help chat and ask for a review. If you paid by credit card and the order never came, ask your card issuer about a chargeback.
