How to File a Canadian Tire Complaint

Published October 25, 2026 · 4 min read
1-800-387-8803
Canadian Tire Corporate Customer Relations (French: 1-800-565-3356)

Canadian Tire stores are run by local Dealers, so a store complaint starts with the store manager. If that fails, Corporate Customer Relations takes it from there. Online orders, roadside assistance and Triangle Rewards each have their own line.

Step 2: Put It in Writing

For an auto service complaint, keep the work order and invoice and ask for a written explanation of the repair. That paper trail matters if you escalate.

Say what happened, when, what you already tried and exactly what you want back. Our complaint letter template shows the six parts every complaint needs.

Step 3: If They Say No

  • Refund refused or misleading sale: file with your provincial consumer office. In Ontario that is Consumer Protection Ontario, 1-800-889-9768, weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Paid by credit card and the item never came or was not as described: ask your card issuer about a chargeback. See our chargeback guide.
  • Larger amounts: Small Claims Court. Ontario's limit is $50,000. See the Small Claims guide.

Auto repair problem in Ontario? Your rights are in the Car Dealer and Mechanic Complaint Guide.

Frequently Asked

What is the Canadian Tire customer service number?

Corporate Customer Relations is 1-800-387-8803 (French 1-800-565-3356). For canadiantire.ca orders call 1-866-746-7287.

How do I complain about Canadian Tire auto service?

Talk to the service manager first with your work order and invoice. If that fails, call Corporate Customer Relations. Ontario repair rules are in our mechanic complaint guide.

My Triangle Rewards points are missing.

Call Triangle Rewards at 1-800-226-8473 with your card number and the receipts for the missing purchases.
