Hospital Complaint in Vancouver? Start With the Patient Care Quality Office

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: contact the Patient Care Quality Office (PCQO) of the health authority that runs the hospital. For Vancouver Coastal Health, call 1-877-993-9199. If you are unhappy with how it was handled, you can ask the Patient Care Quality Review Board to look at it.

Step 1: The Patient Care Quality Office

Each BC health authority has its own PCQO. It takes concerns about your own care or a loved one's care. Vancouver Coastal Health: toll-free 1-877-993-9199.

Call or write. Hours differ between listings, so confirm before you call.

Hospital run by another authority?

The hospital's own website says which authority runs it. Other PCQOs: Fraser Health 1-877-880-8823, Northern Health 1-877-677-7715, Provincial Health Services Authority 1-888-875-3256.

Step 2: The Review Board

If you are unhappy with how the PCQO handled it, ask the Patient Care Quality Review Board to review it. It is independent of the health authority.

The BC Ombudsperson suggests trying the health authority directly first.

↑ Back to sections

If it is about a doctor or nurse

A doctor's conduct is handled by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC. A nurse's conduct is handled by the BC College of Nurses and Midwives.

Province-wide steps are in our healthcare complaint guide.

Frequently Asked

Who do I complain to about a Vancouver hospital?

The Patient Care Quality Office of the health authority that runs it. For Vancouver Coastal Health, call 1-877-993-9199.

What if the PCQO does not fix it?

You can ask the Patient Care Quality Review Board to review how the concern was handled. It is independent of the health authority.

Does the PCQO cover a loved one's care?

Yes. It takes concerns about your own care or a loved one's care.

Where does a complaint about a nurse go?

The BC College of Nurses and Midwives. A complaint about a doctor goes to the College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC.
