Hospital Complaint in Montreal? File With the Complaints Commissioner

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: file with the service quality and complaints commissioner of the establishment that gave the care. Free help filing is available from a CAAP (1-877-767-2227). If you are unhappy with the result, go to the Protecteur du citoyen at 1-800-463-5070.

Step 1: The complaints commissioner

In Quebec, you file with the service quality and complaints commissioner of the establishment involved, such as your regional CIUSSS. A complaint can be verbal or written. The process is confidential and you can stop at any time.

The commissioner can also help you word the complaint.

Free help: the CAAP

The Centres d'assistance et d'accompagnement aux plaintes (CAAP) help you file for free. Phone: 1-877-767-2227. The establishment's users' committee can also help.

For a private establishment, ask the staff or a CAAP which rules apply.

Step 2: The Protecteur du citoyen

If you did not get the result you hoped for, you can complain to the Quebec Ombudsman, the Protecteur du citoyen: 1-800-463-5070.

If it is about a doctor or nurse

Complaints about professionals who belong to a professional order, such as doctors, follow a separate route through that order.

Frequently Asked

Who do I complain to about a Montreal hospital?

The service quality and complaints commissioner of the establishment that gave the care. You can ask a CAAP for free help at 1-877-767-2227.

Can I complain verbally?

Yes. A complaint to the commissioner can be verbal or written.

What if I disagree with the commissioner's answer?

You can complain to the Protecteur du citoyen (Quebec Ombudsman) at 1-800-463-5070.

Is the process confidential?

Yes, and you can stop it at any time.
