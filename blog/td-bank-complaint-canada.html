TD Bank Complaint in Canada? Steps, the TD Ombudsman and OBSI

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: follow TD's complaint steps first. The TD Ombudsman's Office is at 1-888-361-0319. If you are not satisfied, or 56 days have passed, you can go to OBSI at 1-888-451-4519.

Quick steps

  1. Talk to your branch or the TD team you dealt with. Say what happened and what you want, and get a reference number.
  2. Not fixed? Ask them to escalate, or contact TD's Senior Customer Complaints Office yourself.
  3. Still unresolved? Write to the TD Ombudsman: td.ombudsman@td.com, 1-888-361-0319 or 416-982-4884.
  4. Not satisfied, or 56 days since you complained? Go to the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments (OBSI), free: 1-888-451-4519, within 180 days of TD's final response.
  5. OBSI must give its written conclusion within 120 days of having all the information it needs.

Step 1: TD's complaint process

TD has a step-by-step complaint process. Clients must complete Step 1 and Step 2 before the TD Ombudsman can review the case.

TD's Senior Customer Complaints Office handles escalations: 1-888-361-0319.

Step 2: The TD Ombudsman

The Office of the Ombudsman handles issues left unresolved after the internal steps. Phone: 1-888-361-0319. Email: td.ombudsman@td.com.

What the TD Ombudsman can and cannot do: it can review your case and recommend a fix, but it is an office inside TD, not an outside body; for an independent review, go to OBSI, whose recommendations are not binding either.

Step 3: OBSI

If you are not satisfied with how TD handled it, or 56 days have passed since you complained, you can escalate to OBSI. It is free: 1-888-451-4519, ombudsman@obsi.ca.

More: OBSI, Canada's banking ombudsman.

What to put in your complaint

  • Who and when. Account or card details, dates, names of agents.
  • What happened. Short facts, in order.
  • What you want. One clear request.
  • Proof. Bills, statements, emails, reference numbers.

Keep a copy of everything you send.

Frequently Asked

How do I complain about TD Bank?

Follow TD's complaint steps, then the TD Ombudsman's Office at 1-888-361-0319. After that, or after 56 days, you can go to OBSI at 1-888-451-4519.

When can I go to OBSI about TD?

If you are not satisfied with TD's handling, or 56 days have passed since you made the complaint.

Do I have to see the TD Ombudsman first?

Not necessarily for OBSI. But the TD Ombudsman requires you to complete Steps 1 and 2 of TD's process first.

Is OBSI free?

Yes. It is free for consumers and independent of the banks.

How long do I have to take a TD complaint to OBSI?

180 calendar days after TD's final written response. OBSI is free and can recommend compensation up to $350,000.
