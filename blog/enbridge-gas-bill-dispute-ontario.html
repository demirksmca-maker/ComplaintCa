Enbridge Gas Bill Dispute: How to Challenge a High or Catch-Up Bill

Published November 2, 2026 · 5 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: call Customer Care, ask for a supervisor if that fails, then file with Enbridge’s Ombuds Office, and last of all the Ontario Energy Board (1-877-632-2727). A “catch-up” bill is a charge that makes up for earlier use that was estimated or missed, and it is fair to ask exactly what period it covers and how it was worked out.

What is a catch-up bill?

If a meter is not read for a while, the bill is based on an estimate. When a real reading finally arrives, the difference can land on one bill. Complaints about meter reading and large catch-up bills have been reported in Ontario, which is why the first questions below are about reads, not about the amount.

What should I ask Enbridge before I dispute anything?

  • Was each of the past bills estimated or read?
  • What dates does the catch-up charge cover?
  • What were the meter readings used, and when were they taken?
  • Can I see a breakdown of the amount?
  • Can the charge be spread over a payment plan while we sort it out?

Write the answers down with the date and the agent’s name. If an answer is unclear, ask for it in writing.

How does the Ombuds Office work?

Enbridge describes its Ombuds Office as the office of last resort inside the company. It looks at all sides and decides whether the process and the outcome were fair and reasonable. Enbridge says submissions that did not follow the complaint process, or that are just general questions, will not get a response. Use its online complaint checker on enbridgegas.com to see whether the office can take your case, and attach all your documents.

When do I go to the OEB?

The Ontario Energy Board regulates natural gas in Ontario. It asks you to try the company first. If the Ombuds Office or supervisor cannot settle it, file with the OEB’s consumer line (1-877-632-2727) with your account number, contact details and a short timeline of what you and Enbridge said.

A sample message you can adapt

“My Enbridge Gas bill dated [date] includes a charge of [amount] described as [wording on bill]. Please confirm in writing which dates it covers, whether earlier bills were estimated and the meter readings used. I am disputing this charge until I receive that information, and I would like to discuss a payment arrangement for any undisputed part.”

Ask whether you should keep paying the undisputed part while the dispute is open, and get the answer in writing.

Frequently Asked

What is the Enbridge Gas customer service number?

1-877-362-7434 for billing and gas meter questions. Hours are Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern. The busiest times are weekdays 11:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.

What is a catch-up bill?

A bill that includes charges for earlier use that was estimated or not billed. Ask which dates it covers and which meter readings were used.

What is the Enbridge Gas Ombuds Office?

An office of last resort inside Enbridge. It checks whether the process and outcome were fair and reasonable, after you have spoken with a supervisor.

Can I complain to the Ontario Energy Board about Enbridge?

Yes, after trying to resolve it with the company. The OEB’s consumer line is 1-877-632-2727.
