How to File a DoorDash Complaint in Canada

Published October 25, 2026 · 4 min read
855-431-0459
DoorDash customer support in Canada, 24/7 (chat also available)

DoorDash says its Self-Help in the app gives the same options as a live agent: a refund, credit or redelivery. Start there. Phone and chat support are open 24/7.

Step 1: Where to Complain

ChannelContact
In the appOrders → the order → Help → Missing or incorrect items
Phone855-431-0459 (24/7)
Websitehelp.doordash.com

Step 2: Put It in Writing

Report it the same day and add photos. Note whether you want a refund to your card or DoorDash credit; credit only works on DoorDash.

Say what happened, when, what you already tried and exactly what you want back. Our complaint letter template shows the six parts every complaint needs.

Step 3: If They Say No

  • App refused a refund for something you did not get: if you paid by credit card, ask your card issuer about a chargeback. Send the app's chat history with it.
  • Food made you sick: report the restaurant to your local public health unit, not only the app.
  • Pattern of unfair charges or fees: file with your provincial consumer office (Ontario: Consumer Protection Ontario, 1-800-889-9768).

Frequently Asked

What is the DoorDash customer service number in Canada?

DoorDash lists 855-431-0459 for customers in Canada. Chat and phone support are available 24/7.

How do I get a DoorDash refund for a missing item?

Open the order in the app, tap Help, choose Missing or incorrect items and follow the steps. You can be offered a refund, credit or redelivery.

DoorDash only gave me credit. Can I get my money back?

Ask support for a refund to your original payment method. If they refuse and the order never came, ask your card issuer about a chargeback.
