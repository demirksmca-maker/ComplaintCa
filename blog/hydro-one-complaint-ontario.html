Hydro One Complaint: Customer Service, Ombudsman and the OEB

Published November 2, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: there are three steps. Hydro One customer service first, then the Hydro One Ombudsman (1-844-608-8756), then the Ontario Energy Board (1-877-632-2727). Each step expects you to have tried the one before it. Here is what to say and bring at each stage, so you do not have to repeat yourself.

What should I have ready before I call?

  • Your account number and the bill in dispute
  • The exact charge you are questioning and what you think is right
  • Dates of past bills for comparison (a normal month next to the odd one)
  • Any earlier call dates, names and reference numbers

Tip: look at whether the bill was estimated or read from the meter. A bill that jumps after several estimated months is a common cause of shock bills, and it is a fair question to ask.

What do I say to customer service?

Be specific and ask for a result, not an apology:

“My bill dated [date] shows [amount], compared with about [usual amount] in other months. Please tell me whether it was estimated or read, which dates it covers and how the charge was calculated. If it is wrong, I would like it corrected and a written confirmation.”

Ask for a reference number at the end of every call. If the first agent cannot help, ask to speak to a supervisor or to have the file reviewed by the complaints team.

When do I go to the Hydro One Ombudsman?

The Ombudsman is for people who have gone through Hydro One’s complaint process and are still not satisfied with the outcome. Contact the office toll-free at 1-844-608-8756. Explain what you asked for, what Hydro One answered and why you think it is unfair or wrong, and attach the bills and reference numbers.

When do I go to the Ontario Energy Board?

The OEB regulates electricity companies in Ontario. It asks you to raise the problem with the company first. If it is not resolved, you can file with the OEB’s consumer line at 1-877-632-2727 (416-314-2455 in the Greater Toronto Area), or through its website. Have your contact details, account number and a summary of what you and Hydro One said to each other. If the company fixes the problem, the OEB treats the complaint as resolved and closes the file.

Frequently Asked

What is the Hydro One customer service number?

1-888-664-9376, Monday to Friday 7:30 a.m. to 8 p.m., according to Hydro One’s contact page.

What is the Hydro One Ombudsman’s phone number?

1-844-608-8756 (toll-free). The Ombudsman is for complaints that were not resolved through Hydro One’s own complaint process.

Can I complain to the Ontario Energy Board about Hydro One?

Yes, after you have tried to resolve it with Hydro One. The OEB’s consumer line is 1-877-632-2727.

What if I think my bill was wrongly estimated?

Ask Hydro One whether the bill was estimated or read, which dates it covers and how it was calculated, and ask for a correction in writing if it is wrong.
