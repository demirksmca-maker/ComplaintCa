Purolator, UPS or FedEx Lost Your Package? Claim Deadlines

Published November 16, 2026 · Updated November 16, 2026 · 5 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: if you bought the item, tell the seller first, because the sender usually holds the contract with the courier. If you shipped it, open a trace and file a written claim before the deadline: 9 months for a lost parcel with Purolator or FedEx, 60 days with UPS. Damage claims can be as short as 21 days from delivery.

Shortest window in this guide: a claim for damaged or missing contents with FedEx, and some damage claims with Purolator, counted from delivery. Lost-parcel windows are longer.

Are you the buyer or the sender?

You areDo this
Buyer from an online storeWrite to the store and ask for a reship or a refund. The store is the courier's customer.
Sender who paid for the labelOpen a trace and file the claim yourself.
Receiver of a gift or a private saleSend the sender the tracking number and ask them to file.

This matters most with Purolator. Its terms say only the party that is contractually obligated to pay Purolator for the shipment can file a claim.

In Ontario, an online order that does not arrive within 30 days of the promised date can be cancelled in writing. Steps: online order late in Ontario.

Claim deadlines: Purolator, UPS and FedEx

CourierLost or not deliveredDamaged
Purolator9 months from the shipment date21 or 60 days from delivery, depending on the type of damage
UPS60 days (count from the pickup date to be safe)60 days after delivery
FedEx9 months from the shipment date21 calendar days from delivery

Who is allowed to file:

  • Purolator: the party that paid Purolator for the shipment.
  • UPS: the shipper or the receiver. UPS prefers the shipper.
  • FedEx: the sender, the recipient or a third party.

These are for regular parcels shipped within Canada, taken from each courier's Canadian terms and claims pages in November 2026. Freight and international air shipments have different limits. Terms change, so file early and confirm the date on the courier's site.

How to file the claim

  1. Open a trace first. Purolator: use the Virtual Assistant on purolator.com or call 1 888 SHIP-123 (1-888-744-7123). UPS: a lost-package claim can start 24 hours after the expected delivery time has passed. FedEx: use the claims page on fedex.com.
  2. Put the claim in writing. Give the tracking number (Purolator calls it a PIN), where it was shipped from and to, the ship date and the amount you are claiming.
  3. Attach proof of value. A receipt or invoice. For damage, add photos of the item and the packaging.
  4. Keep the item and all packaging until the claim is finished. The courier may want to inspect them.
  5. Write down the claim number. FedEx says most claims are resolved 5 to 7 business days after they are submitted.

Purolator shipment damaged and worth more than $500? Use its claim form or call 1-800-461-0540.

How much will they pay? Each courier caps its liability unless a higher value was declared, and paid for, when the parcel was shipped. Check the shipping receipt for the declared value before you name an amount.

If the courier refuses or does not answer

The Canada Post Ombudsman only reviews Canada Post. For a private courier, these are your next options:

  • The seller. If you were the buyer, your agreement is with the store that sold you the item, so ask the store for the refund first.
  • A card chargeback. If you paid by credit card and the seller will not help, ask your card issuer. See the chargeback guide.
  • Small claims court. For amounts that justify the filing fee. See the Small Claims guide.

Parcel sent by Canada Post instead? The process is different: Canada Post lost package. Got a “delivery failed” text you did not expect? Do not click it. Check it with the scam-check guide.

Frequently Asked

Who files the claim for a lost package, the sender or the receiver?

It depends on the courier. Purolator accepts a claim only from the party that paid for the shipment. UPS lets the shipper or receiver start one but prefers the shipper. FedEx accepts the sender, the recipient or a third party.

How long do I have to file a Purolator claim?

9 months from the shipment date for a lost or undelivered parcel. Damage claims are shorter: 21 or 60 days from delivery, depending on the type of damage.

What is the UPS claim deadline in Canada?

60 days. UPS Canada's terms say it must be notified within 60 days, and claims made later are waived.

What is the FedEx claim deadline in Canada?

21 calendar days from delivery for damaged or missing contents, and 9 months from the shipment date for a parcel that was not delivered or was delivered to the wrong place.

Is there an ombudsman for Purolator, UPS or FedEx?

No. The Canada Post Ombudsman only reviews Canada Post. After a refused courier claim, your options are the seller, a credit card chargeback or small claims court.
