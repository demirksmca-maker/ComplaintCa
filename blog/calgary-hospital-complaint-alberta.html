Hospital Complaint in Calgary? Start With AHS Patient Relations

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: contact Alberta Health Services (AHS) Patient Relations at 1-855-550-2555. If the concern is not resolved, the Alberta Health Advocate offices can help with concerns about treatment.

In This Guide
→1: AHS→2: Advocate→Put It in Writing→About a Doctor→FAQ→Sources

Step 1: AHS Patient Relations

AHS runs Alberta's main hospitals. Patient Relations takes concerns about care. Toll-free: 1-855-550-2555.

Have the patient's name, the hospital and unit, the dates, and what happened ready.

Step 2: Health Advocate offices

The Office of the Alberta Health Advocates includes the Health Advocate and the Mental Health Patient Advocate. Start from the Alberta government complaint page, listed under Sources.

Check that page for the current phone number before you call.

Put it in writing

  • Who and where. Patient, hospital, unit, dates.
  • What happened. Short facts, in order.
  • What you want. One clear request.
  • Proof. Discharge papers, notes, emails.

If it is about a doctor

A doctor's conduct is handled by the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA), not by the hospital.

Frequently Asked

Who do I complain to about a Calgary hospital?

Start with Alberta Health Services Patient Relations at 1-855-550-2555.

What is the Alberta Health Advocate?

The Office of the Alberta Health Advocates includes the Health Advocate and the Mental Health Patient Advocate. They help with concerns about treatment in the health system.

Where does a complaint about a doctor go?

To the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Alberta (CPSA).

Do I need a lawyer to complain?

No. A patient concern or an advocate complaint does not need a lawyer.
