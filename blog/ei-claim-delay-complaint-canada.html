EI Claim Delayed or Denied? How to Complain and Appeal in Canada

Published November 23, 2026 · Updated November 23, 2026 · 4 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: Service Canada aims to pay EI, or tell you why not, within 28 days of your application. If it is late, check your account and call EI, then complain to the Office for Client Satisfaction: 1-866-506-6806. If EI is denied, ask for a reconsideration within 30 days. Since April 1, 2026, the next appeal goes to the EI Board of Appeal.

Quick steps

  1. Check your claim in My Service Canada Account. Send anything missing, such as your Record of Employment.
  2. Past 28 days with no answer? Call Service Canada about EI and ask what is holding the claim. Note the date and the agent.
  3. Service problem, such as long delays or no call back? Complain to the Service Canada Office for Client Satisfaction: 1-866-506-6806.
  4. Disagree with a decision? Ask for a reconsideration within 30 days of the decision. It is free.
  5. Still disagree? Appeal to the EI Board of Appeal within 30 days of the reconsideration decision.

The 28-day standard

Service Canada's standard is to send your first EI payment, or a notice that you will not be paid, within 28 days of your application. Its target is to meet this 80% of the time.

The 28 days assume you are eligible and sent everything needed. Missing documents or a complex file can make it longer.

Complain: Office for Client Satisfaction

The Office for Client Satisfaction takes complaints about Service Canada's service.

  • Phone: 1-866-506-6806 (TTY 1-866-506-6803)
  • Mail: Office for Client Satisfaction, 140 Promenade du Portage, Floor 3, Mail Drop 318, Gatineau QC K1A 0J9

Try the Service Canada agent or a manager first. This office does not replace a reconsideration or appeal: it cannot change an EI decision.

Denied? Ask for a reconsideration

If you disagree with an EI decision, ask Service Canada to look at it again. This is called a request for reconsideration.

  • Deadline: 30 days after the decision was communicated to you.
  • Cost: free.
  • Late? Service Canada can still accept it if you have a reasonable explanation.

Explain why the decision is wrong and attach proof: pay stubs, emails, a letter from your employer.

Appeal: the EI Board of Appeal

Since April 1, 2026, the Employment Insurance Board of Appeal hears first-level EI appeals. It replaced the Social Security Tribunal General Division for new cases.

  • Deadline: 30 days from the reconsideration decision.
  • Panels: three members: a chair, one from employers and one from workers.
  • Next step: the Social Security Tribunal Appeal Division.

Appeals filed with the Social Security Tribunal before March 31, 2026 stay there.

Frequently Asked

How long does an EI claim take?

Service Canada aims to pay, or send a non-payment notice, within 28 days of your application, if you are eligible and sent everything needed.

Who do I complain to about Service Canada?

The Office for Client Satisfaction: 1-866-506-6806. Try the agent or a manager first.

How long do I have to ask for an EI reconsideration?

30 days after the decision was communicated to you. It is free.

Who hears EI appeals now?

Since April 1, 2026, the EI Board of Appeal. You have 30 days from the reconsideration decision.

Can the Office for Client Satisfaction change my EI decision?

No. It handles service complaints. To change a decision, use reconsideration and then appeal.
