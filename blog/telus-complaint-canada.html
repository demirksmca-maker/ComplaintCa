Telus Complaint in Canada? Your Options, in Order

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: raise it with Telus first and keep every reference number. If you cannot resolve it, the free CCTS can help: 1-888-221-1687. Check the CCTS site for its current filing rules.

In This Guide
→1: Telus First→2: CCTS→Rules May Change→Write It Down→FAQ→Sources

Step 1: Raise it with Telus

Contact Telus by phone, chat or your online account. Ask for an itemized review of the charge or a clear fix for the problem. Note the date, the agent's name and a reference number.

For Telus's current consumer escalation steps, check telus.com. The escalation form we found online is for business customers.

↑ Back to sections

Step 2: The CCTS

The CCTS is free for consumers. Phone: 1-888-221-1687. Customers do not currently have to finish every step of a carrier's internal escalation before contacting it.

Service providers must tell customers about the CCTS after the second escalation of an unresolved problem.

The rules may change

Telus has asked the CRTC to change how telecom complaints are filed. The CRTC had not decided when we checked. Confirm the current requirements on the CCTS website before you file.

What to put in your complaint

  • Who and when. Account or card details, dates, names of agents.
  • What happened. Short facts, in order.
  • What you want. One clear request.
  • Proof. Bills, statements, emails, reference numbers.

Keep a copy of everything you send.

Frequently Asked

How do I complain about Telus?

Contact Telus first and keep reference numbers. If you cannot resolve it, file a free complaint with the CCTS at 1-888-221-1687.

Do I need to finish Telus's escalation steps before the CCTS?

Not currently, according to recent reports. A CRTC proceeding may change this, so check the CCTS site.

Does the CCTS cost anything?

No. It is free for consumers.
