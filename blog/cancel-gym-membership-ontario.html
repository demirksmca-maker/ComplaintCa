How to Cancel a Gym Membership in Ontario: Your Rights

Published November 16, 2026 · Updated November 16, 2026 · 4 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: in Ontario you can cancel a gym membership for any reason within 10 days of receiving your written contract, and the gym has 15 days to refund you. After that, check three things: the contract cannot run longer than one year, it cannot renew without notice, and a contract missing required details can be cancelled within one year.

10 days
Cooling-off period to cancel an Ontario gym contract for any reason, counted from the day you receive your written copy.

The 10-day cooling-off period

Ontario's Consumer Protection Act calls prepaid memberships personal development services. That covers gyms and fitness clubs, sports clubs, martial arts schools and dance classes.

  • You can cancel within 10 days of receiving a written copy of your contract.
  • You do not need a reason.
  • You can cancel even if you already used the gym during those days.
  • A contract cannot take this right away, whatever it says.

After you cancel, the business has 15 days to return your money.

After 10 days: three ways out

  1. The contract is missing required information. It must show the total cost, the payment schedule and the cancellation and renewal terms. If something required is missing, you can cancel within one year.
  2. The contract is longer than one year. Every gym contract in Ontario must end after one year. A gym cannot lock you into a two- or three-year term. It can only renew with proper notice (next section).
  3. The contract's own cancellation clause. Outside these rules, the contract's terms apply. Read the cancellation section and follow it exactly, in writing.

Gyms must also offer a monthly payment option, so you never have to pay a full year up front. Paying monthly limits what you can lose if the club closes or the dispute drags on.

Auto-renewal: the 30-to-90-day notice

A gym can renew your membership only if it sends you a renewal notice at least 30 days and no more than 90 days before the contract ends.

What happenedResult
You got a valid notice and did nothingThe club can renew and bill you
You got a valid notice and said no in writingThe membership ends on the contract date
No notice, but you were renewed and billedThe renewal is not a valid contract. You may give notice of cancellation

Check your email, spam folder and mail for a notice before you write. If there was none, say so in your letter and ask for the charges after the end date to be refunded.

Sample cancellation letter

Cancel in writing so you can prove the date. Email it, hand-deliver it and get a signed receipt, or use registered mail.

“[Date]
To: [gym name and address]

I am cancelling my membership agreement dated [date], member number [number], under Ontario's Consumer Protection Act.

[Choose one:] I received my written contract on [date] and I am cancelling within the 10-day cooling-off period. / My membership was renewed without the renewal notice the Act requires.

Please stop all payments now and refund $[amount] within 15 days. Please confirm in writing.

[Name, address, email, phone]”

Keep a copy of the letter, the contract and your bank or card statements showing each charge.

If the gym keeps charging you

  1. Write once more with a copy of your cancellation and the dates of the new charges.
  2. File with Consumer Protection Ontario at 416-326-8800 or toll-free 1-800-889-9768. Include your letters, the contract and the statements.
  3. Dispute the charges with your card issuer. See the chargeback guide, and what to do if a chargeback is denied.

These rules are Ontario's. Other provinces have their own, so check with your provincial consumer office if you joined a gym elsewhere.

Frequently Asked

Can I cancel a gym membership in Ontario?

Yes. You can cancel for any reason within 10 days of receiving your written contract, even if you already used the gym. Give the gym written notice.

How long does a gym have to refund me in Ontario?

15 days after you cancel during the cooling-off period.

How long can a gym contract last in Ontario?

One year at most. All contracts must end after one year, although they may be renewed with proper notice.

Can a gym renew my membership automatically in Ontario?

Only if it sends you a renewal notice at least 30 days and no more than 90 days before the contract ends. A renewal without that notice is not a valid contract, and you may cancel it.

Do I have to pay for a full year up front?

No. Gyms in Ontario must offer a monthly payment option.
