How to Cancel a Gym Membership in Ontario: The 10-Day Rule, Renewals and Refunds

Published October 8, 2026 · 5 min read
10 days
to cancel a new gym contract in Ontario, counted from the day you receive a written copy. No reason needed.

Ontario's Consumer Protection Act treats a prepaid gym membership as a "personal development service", with special rules. You can walk away from a new contract, a gym can't lock you in for more than a year, and an automatic renewal without proper notice doesn't count.

Is Your Gym Covered?

The rules apply to gyms, sports clubs, martial arts and dance classes when your contract requires $50 or more in advance. They don't apply to:

  • Not-for-profit or charitable clubs, such as the YMCA
  • Member-owned or cooperative clubs
  • Clubs run or funded by a municipality, the province or its agencies
  • Fitness areas that are only part of another service, like a spa

Your Rights, Step by Step

1The 10-day cooling-off period. Cancel within 10 days of receiving a written copy of your contract, for any reason. You can use the gym during those 10 days.

2One year, maximum. All gym contracts must end after one year.

3Renewals need notice. The gym must send you a renewal notice 30 to 90 days before your contract ends, with a copy showing all changes. Without it, the renewal isn't valid: you can cancel and ask for any money paid after the original contract ended.

4Missing information? One year to cancel. If your contract leaves out required details, such as names and addresses, the services, cancellation and renewal terms, the total cost, or the start and end dates, you can cancel within one year.

5Cancel in writing. Use email, registered mail, or hand delivery with a signed receipt, so you can prove the date. If you cancel by phone, write down the date and details of the call.

Fees the Law Limits

  • Initiation fee: can't be more than twice the total annual membership fee.
  • Monthly payments: the gym must offer them. They can cost up to 25% more than paying upfront.
Gym won't accept your cancellation? Write to the business first, then file a complaint with Consumer Protection Ontario online, or call 1-800-889-9768 (416-326-8800 in Toronto), 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday. If the gym keeps charging your credit card after a valid cancellation, ask your card issuer about a chargeback.

Ontario passed a new Consumer Protection Act in 2023. It takes effect on a date set by proclamation, and its rules may change some of these details. Check ontario.ca before relying on a specific rule.

Send a written cancellation that states the date, your membership number and the rule you're relying on.

Frequently Asked

Can I cancel a gym membership in Ontario within 10 days?

Yes, for most gym contracts that require $50 or more in advance. You can cancel within 10 days of receiving a written copy of your contract, and you don't need a reason. Cancel in writing so you can prove the date.

Can a gym renew my membership automatically in Ontario?

Not without notice. All gym contracts must end after one year. To renew, the gym must send you a notice 30 to 90 days before the contract expires, with a copy of the contract showing all changes. A renewal without proper notice is not valid.

What if my gym contract is missing information?

You can cancel within one year if the contract is missing required information, such as the names and addresses of both parties, a full description of the services, the cancellation and renewal terms, the total cost, or the start and end dates.

Do these rules apply to the YMCA or a city gym?

No. Not-for-profit and charitable clubs such as the YMCA, member-owned or cooperative clubs, clubs run or funded by a municipality, the province or its agencies, and fitness areas that are only part of another service, like a spa, are not covered.
