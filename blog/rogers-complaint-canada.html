Rogers Complaint in Canada? Who to Contact, in Order

Published November 16, 2026 · Updated November 16, 2026 · 4 min read
By Kasım Demir · Founder, ComplaintCA

Short answer: contact a Rogers Specialist first, then ask for your concern to go to management. If Rogers still does not fix it, file a free complaint with the Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS) at 1-888-221-1687. Rogers then has 20 calendar days to resolve it.

20 days
Calendar days Rogers has to resolve a complaint once the CCTS accepts it and sends it back. After that, the CCTS looks into it further.

Step 1: Contact a Rogers Specialist

Rogers' own "Resolve a concern" page names a Specialist as the first step. You can ask for a callback, visit a store, phone, or write to:

Rogers Communications, Share a concern, 333 Bloor St. E, Toronto, ON M4W 1G9

Have your account number, the bill dates, the exact amount in dispute and what you were promised. A clear opening saves a second call:

“My account number is [number]. My [month] bill shows a charge of $[amount] for [item] that I did not agree to. Please remove it and confirm in writing. Can I have a reference number for this conversation?”

Write down the date, the time, the agent's name and the reference number. You will need them in Step 3.

Step 2: Ask for it to go to management

If the Specialist cannot fix it, say plainly that you want the concern escalated to the management team. That is the next level Rogers describes.

Rogers' Terms of Service also name an Office of the President above management. We did not find a public phone number for it, so ask the manager handling your file how to send your complaint there.

  • Keep paying the part of the bill you do not dispute. Dispute only the wrong amount.
  • Former Shaw customer? Rogers has a separate "Resolve a concern: Rogers Together with Shaw" page. Start there.

Step 3: File with the CCTS

The Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS) is the independent body for phone, internet and TV disputes. It is free. File online at ccts-cprst.ca or call 1-888-221-1687 (TTY 1-844-713-3010), Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern.

Rogers' page says you must first try to resolve the problem with Rogers directly, so attach proof that you tried: dates, names and reference numbers from Steps 1 and 2.

What happens after you file

  1. The CCTS checks that your complaint is within its mandate.
  2. It refers the complaint back to Rogers, which has 20 calendar days to resolve it with you.
  3. If you are still not satisfied, the CCTS investigates whether Rogers reasonably followed the terms of your service.
  4. It can recommend a resolution. If that is not accepted, it can issue a decision. The decision binds Rogers only if you accept it, so you can still say no.

Answer every message from Rogers and the CCTS in writing during this time, and keep copies. Complaints about what is shown on TV (programming, not billing) go to the CRTC instead.

Frequently Asked

How do I file a complaint with Rogers?

Contact a Rogers Specialist by phone, callback, in a store or by mail (Rogers Communications, Share a concern, 333 Bloor St. E, Toronto, ON M4W 1G9). If that does not fix it, ask for management, then file with the CCTS.

How long does Rogers have to respond to a CCTS complaint?

20 calendar days. That is the time Rogers has to resolve the complaint after the CCTS refers it back.

Is there a deadline to complain to the CCTS about Rogers?

Yes. The CCTS Procedural Code sets a one-year limit, so file as soon as Rogers fails to fix the problem.

How much compensation can the CCTS award against Rogers?

Up to $5,000. It can also recommend a corrected bill, an explanation or an apology.

Does it cost anything to complain about Rogers to the CCTS?

No. The CCTS is free for consumers across Canada. Phone: 1-888-221-1687.
