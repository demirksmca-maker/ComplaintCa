How to File a Walmart Canada Complaint

Published October 25, 2026 · 4 min read
1-800-328-0402
Walmart Canada customer care, 7 days a week, 7 a.m. to 1 a.m. ET

For a store problem, start with the store manager. For walmart.ca orders, call customer care or use the help page. Walmart Canada also lists separate lines for gift cards and protection plans.

Step 2: Put It in Writing

Keep the receipt or order number, take photos of a damaged item and note the name of anyone you spoke to. Ask for the store manager before you call head office.

Say what happened, when, what you already tried and exactly what you want back. Our complaint letter template shows the six parts every complaint needs.

Step 3: If They Say No

  • Refund refused or misleading sale: file with your provincial consumer office. In Ontario that is Consumer Protection Ontario, 1-800-889-9768, weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.
  • Paid by credit card and the item never came or was not as described: ask your card issuer about a chargeback. See our chargeback guide.
  • Larger amounts: Small Claims Court. Ontario's limit is $50,000. See the Small Claims guide.

Frequently Asked

What is the Walmart Canada customer service number?

Call 1-800-328-0402, 7 days a week from 7 a.m. to 1 a.m. Eastern. TTY service is also available.

Walmart refused my return. What can I do?

Ask for the store manager and the written return policy. If you think the refusal breaks the policy or the law, call customer care, then your provincial consumer office.

My walmart.ca order never arrived.

Report it through your walmart.ca account or call customer care. If you paid by credit card and get no fix, ask your card issuer about a chargeback.
