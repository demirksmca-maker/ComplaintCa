How to File a Walmart Canada Complaint

Published October 25, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: call Walmart Canada customer care at 1-800-328-0402, every day from 7 a.m. to 1 a.m. Eastern. If a store refuses a return, ask the manager for the written return policy; if you believe it breaks the policy or the law, call customer care, then your provincial consumer office.

At Walmart, the quickest remedy sits behind the store manager's desk, not behind a phone queue. Head office only becomes useful once the store has said no. Orders placed on walmart.ca, gift cards and product protection plans each run through separate teams, which is why a single "customer service" call often goes nowhere.

Start with the store, then escalate

For a refund, a pricing mistake or a staff problem at a location, ask for the store manager and explain the problem in a sentence or two. Managers can usually approve exceptions on the spot. Note the date, time and the name of the person who helped you. If the answer is no, ask what the written policy says and where it is posted.

Only then move to the central team: customer care at 1-800-328-0402 is open every day from 7 a.m. to 1 a.m. Eastern, and TTY service is available.

Walmart.ca: who sold it matters

Items on walmart.ca can come from Walmart itself or from third-party sellers on its marketplace. The order page shows which one it is. Walmart handles its own sales directly; for a marketplace item the seller is the first contact, and Walmart's customer care can tell you what protection applies. Screenshot the product page, the seller name and the order status before anything changes.

Gift cards and protection plans

A gift card with a missing balance needs the card number and the receipt where it was bought, so keep both. For a protection plan claim, have the plan number, the proof of purchase and a description of the fault. Because these run on separate lines, mention the product by name so the agent can find the right file.

Building a record that gets results

  1. Write down who you spoke to and when. A running list beats memory.
  2. Photograph damaged items and keep packaging until the dispute is settled.
  3. Confirm any promise by email or through your account so there is something to quote.

When the store and customer care both refuse, move to the outside options: your provincial consumer office (Consumer Protection Ontario, 1-800-889-9768), a chargeback if you paid by credit card (how it works), or Small Claims Court (guide).

Frequently Asked

What is Walmart Canada's customer care number?

1-800-328-0402, every day from 7 a.m. to 1 a.m. Eastern. TTY service is available.

The store refused my return. What now?

Ask the store manager for the written return policy. If you believe the refusal breaks that policy or the law, call customer care, then your provincial consumer office.

Where do I call about a Walmart gift card?

Walmart Canada lists 1-888-537-5503 for gift cards and 1-855-308-7295 for product protection plans.

My walmart.ca order never arrived.

Report it through your walmart.ca account or customer care. If you paid by credit card and nothing is fixed, ask your card issuer about a chargeback.

When the till price is wrong

If an item scans higher than the shelf price, point it out before you leave. Many large Canadian retailers follow the voluntary Scanner Price Accuracy Code, under which a mispriced item may be free if it is $10 or less, or $10 off if it costs more. Ask the cashier or manager whether Walmart applies it, and photograph the shelf label as proof.
