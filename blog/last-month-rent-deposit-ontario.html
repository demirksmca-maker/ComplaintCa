Last Month's Rent Deposit in Ontario: Interest, Illegal Deposits and How to Get Money Back

Published October 8, 2026 · 5 min read

Many Ontario tenants pay "first and last" and never think about the deposit again. But the law limits what a landlord can collect, says exactly what the deposit can be used for, and requires the landlord to pay you interest on it every year.

What a Landlord Can and Can't Collect

DepositAllowed?
Rent deposit (last month's rent)Yes, up to one month's rent
Key depositYes, refundable, no more than the cost of replacing the keys
Damage or security depositNo
Pet depositNo
Required post-dated chequesNo, a landlord can't require them

The Rules, Step by Step

1It's only for your last month. The rent deposit can only be used as rent for your last month (or week) before you move out. It can't be used for anything else, such as repairing damage.

2Interest every year. The landlord must pay you interest on the deposit every year, at the rent increase guideline rate: 2.1% in 2026, 1.9% in 2027. On a $2,000 deposit, 2.1% is $42.

3Top-ups when rent goes up. If your rent increases, the landlord can ask you to raise the deposit by the same amount, and can use the interest it owes you to top it up.

4Interest not paid? You can deduct the interest from your next rent payment, or apply to the Landlord and Tenant Board.

5Deposit not used or returned? If the landlord didn't apply your deposit to your last month and didn't give it back, apply to the LTB on Form T1 within 12 months of the date the landlord kept the money.

Paid an illegal charge? Key money, premiums, fees, commissions and penalties are illegal charges. On Form T1 you can ask for illegal charges you paid in the last 12 months. The LTB can order up to $50,000. Questions: LTB at 1-888-332-3234.

Ask your landlord for your deposit interest in writing, with the year, the rate and the amount.

Frequently Asked

Can a landlord in Ontario charge a damage deposit?

No. In Ontario, the only deposit a landlord can take is a rent deposit of no more than one month's rent (or one week's, for weekly rent), plus a refundable key deposit no higher than the actual cost of replacing the keys. Damage and pet deposits are not allowed.

How much interest does my landlord owe on my last month's rent deposit?

Every year, interest equal to the rent increase guideline in effect when the interest is due: 2.1% in 2026 and 1.9% in 2027. The landlord can pay it to you or add it to your deposit to top it up.

What if my landlord doesn't pay interest on my deposit?

You can deduct the interest from your next rent payment, or apply to the Landlord and Tenant Board with Form T1.

How long do I have to get my deposit back through the LTB?

If the landlord did not use your last month's rent deposit for your last rental period and did not return it, you must apply on Form T1 within 12 months of the date the landlord kept the money. The LTB can order up to $50,000.
