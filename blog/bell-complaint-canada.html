Bell Complaint in Canada? How to Escalate, Step by Step

Published November 3, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: start with Bell Customer Relations. If it is still unresolved, contact the Bell Escalation Team at 1-866-317-3382. After that, the free CCTS can help: 1-888-221-1687.

Step 1: Bell customer service

Start with a customer service representative. Note the date, the agent's name and any reference number.

If the first agent cannot fix it, ask to escalate.

↑ Back to sections

Step 2: Customer Relations and the Escalation Team

Bell's escalation page describes a complaint to the Bell Customer Relations Centre next. If it is still unresolved, contact the Bell Escalation Team at 1-866-317-3382.

That page is labelled for small business, but it says Bell residential customers follow the same escalation process.

↑ Back to sections

Step 3: The CCTS

The Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS) is free for consumers. Phone: 1-888-221-1687, or file online at ccts-cprst.ca.

It asks the provider to try to resolve the issue with you directly. Full steps: the CCTS process.

↑ Back to sections

What to put in your complaint

  • Who and when. Account or card details, dates, names of agents.
  • What happened. Short facts, in order.
  • What you want. One clear request.
  • Proof. Bills, statements, emails, reference numbers.

Keep a copy of everything you send.

Frequently Asked

How do I escalate a complaint with Bell?

Start with customer service, then Bell Customer Relations. If still unresolved, contact the Bell Escalation Team at 1-866-317-3382.

When can I go to the CCTS about Bell?

If you cannot reach a satisfactory resolution with Bell, the CCTS may be able to help. It is free: 1-888-221-1687.

Does the Bell escalation page apply to home customers?

Yes. The page says Bell residential customers follow the same escalation process.

Does it cost anything to complain to the CCTS?

No. The CCTS is free for consumers.
